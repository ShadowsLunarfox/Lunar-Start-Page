# Built-in wallpaper sources

These files replace the former WallpaperFlare images and Komainu video. Wallpaper selection, image and video playback, and custom uploads use the same implementation. The existing preset IDs are retained so previously selected built-in wallpapers point to the new media.

| Bundled file | Source and credit | Preparation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `blue-marble.jpg` | [NASA Blue Marble 2007 West](https://images.nasa.gov/details/GSFC_20171208_Archive_e002131), NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center | Resized and padded with black to 1920 × 1080; JPEG conversion. |
| `lunar-earthset.jpg` | [A Breathtaking Earthset from Orion](https://images.nasa.gov/details/art002e021278), NASA; Artemis II, April 6, 2026 | Center crop to 1920 × 1080; JPEG conversion. |
| `cosmic-cliffs.jpg` | [Cosmic Cliffs, NIRCam and MIRI composite](https://science.nasa.gov/asset/webb/cosmic-cliffs-in-the-carina-nebula-nircam-and-miri-composite-image/), NASA, ESA, CSA, STScI | Center crop to 1920 × 1080; JPEG conversion. This is a processed telescope image. |
| `Mp4/earthset-loop.mp4` | [Goodnight Earth](https://images.nasa.gov/details/art002m1200962241_Goodnight-Earth_mp4), NASA; Artemis II | Seconds 10–26, rotated for a landscape view, resized to 1280 × 720, played forward then backward to make a 32-second loop; 24 fps H.264; audio removed. |

All four wallpapers are bundled locally. Selecting them does not download media or send a request to NASA.
