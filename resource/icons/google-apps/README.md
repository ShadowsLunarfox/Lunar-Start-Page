These are unmodified copies of Google product icons checked on 2026-11-01 against
the [Google Workspace products page](https://workspace.google.com/products/) and
[Google's product directory](https://about.google/products/). Workspace uses the
2026 designs (including Sheets' 2026 Q3 revision); Search, Maps and Photos use the
current gradient artwork. Other products use their currently published official
artwork. Business Profile also uses Google's official icon.

[sources.json](sources.json) records each local filename, official asset URL,
source page, verification date and SHA-256. SVG, PNG and WebP files are bundled
locally; opening Lunar's launcher does not request images from Google. Product
names and marks belong to their respective owners.
